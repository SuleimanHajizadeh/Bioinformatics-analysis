# COMT Catechol-O-Methyltransferase

## Overview
**COMT** catalyzes the transfer of a methyl group from S-adenosylmethionine (SAM) to catecholamines (dopamine, epinephrine, norepinephrine), serving as the primary enzymatic degradation pathway for prefrontal dopamine.

## Val158Met Functional Polymorphism (`rs4680`)
- **A/A (Met/Met - "Worrier")**: Thermolabile enzyme with 3- to 4-fold reduced activity $\to$ high tonic prefrontal dopamine $\to$ superior working memory and executive focus, but increased anxiety and stress sensitivity.
- **G/G (Val/Val - "Warrior")**: Highly active enzyme $\to$ rapid dopamine degradation $\to$ optimal stress resilience and emotional calm under acute pressure.
- **A/G (Val/Met)**: Intermediate phenotype.
