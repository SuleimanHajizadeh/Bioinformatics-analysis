# MK-2206 Allosteric AKT Inhibitor

## Overview
**MK-2206** is an orally active, potent, highly selective allosteric inhibitor of all three AKT isoforms (AKT1, AKT2, AKT3). Unlike ATP-competitive inhibitors, MK-2206 binds the allosteric interface between the Pleckstrin Homology (PH) domain and kinase domain, locking AKT in a closed, catalytically inactive conformation and preventing membrane translocation.

## Biophysical & Docking Profile
- **Target**: Allosteric pocket of [[akt1-kinase]].
- **Calculated Affinity ($\Delta G$)**: $-9.4\text{ kcal/mol}$ via [[autodock-vina]].
- **Key Interacting Residues**:
  - Hydrogen bonding: **Trp80** (PH domain) and **Asp292** (kinase domain activation loop).
  - Hydrophobic contacts: Interdomain pocket residues.
