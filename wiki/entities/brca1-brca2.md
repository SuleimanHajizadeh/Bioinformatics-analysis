# BRCA1 and BRCA2 Tumor Suppressor Genes

## Overview
**BRCA1** (17q21.31) and **BRCA2** (13q13.1) encode essential tumor suppressor proteins maintaining genomic stability by executing error-free homologous recombination repair of DNA double-strand breaks.

## Clinical Relevance in Oncology
- **Hereditary Breast and Ovarian Cancer (HBOC)** syndrome.
- Approximately 70–80% of breast tumors arising in *BRCA1* germline mutation carriers exhibit the [[triple-negative-breast-cancer]] / basal-like phenotype.
- Loss-of-function variants confer exceptional vulnerability to synthetic lethality mediated by PARP inhibitors via [[homologous-recombination-deficiency-brca]].
