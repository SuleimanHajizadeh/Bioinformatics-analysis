# Ipatasertib (GDC-0068) ATP-Competitive AKT Inhibitor

## Overview
**Ipatasertib** (GDC-0068) is an orally bioavailable, highly selective small-molecule ATP-competitive pan-AKT inhibitor. It binds directly to the ATP-binding pocket in the catalytic domain of [[akt1-kinase]], AKT2, and AKT3.

## Biophysical & Clinical Profile
- **Target**: ATP-binding cleft of [[akt1-kinase]].
- **Calculated Affinity ($\Delta G$)**: $-8.2\text{ kcal/mol}$ via [[autodock-vina]] (in comparison to $-9.4\text{ kcal/mol}$ for allosteric inhibitor [[mk-2206]]).
- **PubChem CID**: 46845511.
- **Clinical Trials**: Evaluated in combination with Paclitaxel in the LOTUS (Phase II) and IPATunity130 (Phase III) randomized clinical trials for locally advanced or metastatic [[triple-negative-breast-cancer]] with *PIK3CA*/*AKT1*/*PTEN* genomic alterations.
