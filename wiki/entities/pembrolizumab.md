# Pembrolizumab (Keytruda) Anti-PD-1 Monoclonal Antibody

## Overview
**Pembrolizumab** is a humanized IgG4-kappa monoclonal antibody that targets the Programmed Cell Death Protein 1 (PD-1 / *PDCD1*) receptor on T-cells. It blocks the interaction between PD-1 and its ligands (PD-L1 / *CD274* and PD-L2 / *PDCD1LG2*), reversing T-cell exhaustion and unleashing anti-tumor cytotoxic immunity.

## Role & Clinical Standard in TNBC
- **KEYNOTE-522**: FDA approved for early-stage TNBC in combination with neoadjuvant chemotherapy, significantly improving pathological complete response (pCR) and event-free survival (EFS).
- **KEYNOTE-355**: FDA approved for locally recurrent unresectable or metastatic TNBC whose tumors express PD-L1 ($\text{CPS} \ge 10$).
- Directly connected to computational [[immune-deconvolution-tme]] metrics assessing CD8+ tumor-infiltrating lymphocyte density.
