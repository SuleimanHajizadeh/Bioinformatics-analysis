# CYP1A2 Cytochrome P450 Enzyme

## Overview
**CYP1A2** encodes an enzyme of the cytochrome P450 superfamily involved in the bioactivation and clearance of polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons, heterocyclic amines, caffeine, theophylline, and clozapine.

## Polymorphism Profile (`rs762551`)
- **A/A Genotype (*1F/*1F)**: Highly inducible "Fast Metabolizer" with rapid caffeine clearance and reduced myocardial infarction risk from coffee consumption.
- **A/C or C/C Genotype**: "Slow Metabolizer" with prolonged systemic caffeine half-life.
