# AKT1 Serine/Threonine Kinase (PKB alpha)

## Overview
**AKT1** (RAC-alpha serine/threonine-protein kinase) is a critical central kinase in the PI3K-Akt signaling axis that regulates cell survival, metabolism, angiogenesis, and cell cycle progression. It is frequently hyperactivated in [[triple-negative-breast-cancer]].

## Structural Architecture & Domains
- **Pleckstrin Homology (PH) Domain** (Residues 5–108): Binds phosphatidylinositol-(3,4,5)-trisphosphate ($\text{PIP}_3$) at the plasma membrane.
- **Catalytic Kinase Domain** (Residues 150–408): Transfers phosphate groups from ATP to downstream oncogenic substrates (GSK3, FOXO, BAD, mTORC1).
- **Regulatory C-terminal Hydrophobic Motif** (Residues 409–480): Contains the key activating phosphorylation site Ser473.

## Role as Master Hub in TNBC
- Identified via [[wgcna-coexpression-networks]] as the highest intramodular connectivity node ($k_{\text{in}} = 0.94$) in the oncogenic turquoise module.
- Validated target for allosteric small-molecule inhibition by [[mk-2206]].
